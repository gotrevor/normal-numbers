## ⚠️ Gotchas added this lap

  `Int.floor_add_fract` (and `Int.self_sub_floor`).
- `ext n` on a `Finset` equality can descend into the underlying `List` and produce `List.Mem`
  goals.  Use `apply Finset.ext; intro n; rw [Finset.mem_filter, …]` instead.

  `Finset.mem_filter` then fails to match.

  written as `<`/`≤` conjunctions (`Real.decidableLT` is an instance) — but the enclosing `def`
  must be `noncomputable`.

  `abs_of_nonneg (show (0:ℝ) ≤ e from …)`.
