## Gotchas (this session, all still live — see PENDING_WORK.md for fuller detail)

- **`List.getElem_ofFn` + `congr 1` on `Fin`-coerced indices**: always add
  `simp only [Fin.val_mk]` before `congr 1`, even if the linter flags it
  "unused" — omitting it caused a *nondeterministic* rebuild failure
  (same file, same content, different `omega` outcome across runs). Not
  yet root-caused; treat as a hard rule, not a judgment call.
- **`Nat.add_mul_div_right`/`_left`/`Nat.add_mul_mod_self_right`/`_left`
  all require the addend/divisor in a SPECIFIC factor position** — always
  `#check` the exact lemma statement and explicitly `rw [Nat.add_comm]`
  (or `mul_comm`) to match before applying; guessing `_left` vs `_right`
  wastes a rewrite-fails-silently cycle almost every time.
- **`Finset.card_nbij'`/`Finset.card_eq_sum_card_fiberwise` goals appear
  in `Set.MapsTo`/coe form**: need `simp only [Finset.coe_filter,
  Finset.mem_range, Set.mem_setOf_eq]` (not plain `Finset.mem_filter`) to
  unfold membership after `intro`/`rintro`. `Set.mem_setOf_eq` is
  deprecated (→ `Set.mem_ofPred_eq`) but still works — cosmetic only.
- **Fiber sets from `card_eq_sum_card_fiberwise` are NESTED filters**
  (`{a ∈ {x∈s|p x} | f a=b}`), not flat — when the fiber predicate and the
  base-set predicate both constrain the same variable, flatten via an
  explicit `ext`+`simp`+`tauto` lemma BEFORE applying `card_nbij'` on the
  fiber, or index errors cascade into confusing "more than one
  constructor" / dependent-elimination failures.
