Negatives below are "not found by these searches," not nonexistence proofs.

## Headlines

1. **abc ⇒ digit/normality statement for a specific natural constant: NOT FOUND.**  The prize
   is unclaimed.  Nearest neighbors: Bailey–Borwein–Crandall–Pomerance 2004 (≫ n^{1/d} one-bits
   for degree-d algebraic irrationals — unconditional, no abc); digit-flavored abc consequences
   exist only for integers/rationals (repunits, the Waring chain below).
2. **Non-Wieferich under abc is stuck at `≫ log x`.**  Silverman 1988 (`≫ log x`); Graves–Murty
   2013 (APs, `log x / log log x`); Ding 2019 C.R.A.S. (APs, back to `≫ log x` — current best);
   number-field/algebraic-group extensions at the same strength.  **No density improvement under
   any abc variant, Baker's explicit abc included.  "Wieferich primes have density zero" is not
   a theorem under anything** — only the Crandall–Dilcher–Pomerance 1997 heuristic
   (Prob ≈ 1/p ⇒ ~log log x Wieferich primes; Dorais–Klyve 2011 empirics to 6.7×10¹⁵ agree).
3. **Fermat-quotient distribution: the prime aspect is heuristic-only.**  Rigorous results
   (Heath-Brown; Ostafe–Shparlinski 2010–11) are all base-aspect (fixed p, varying u).  The
   prime-aspect statement our node wants — *`q_p(2)` avoids a prescribed residue for almost all
   p* — has no theorem AND no formal conjecture beyond CDP uniformity; it generalizes the
   Wieferich problem (the r = 0 case).
4. **The one real abc→‖·‖ chain works only for rational bases.**  Mahler 1957 (ineffective);
   S. David via abc (effective floor under ‖(3/2)ᵏ‖, per Waldschmidt's notes §3.6); Laishram
   2015 (ideal Waring for ALL k under Baker's explicit abc); Zudilin 2007 unconditional
   `‖(3/2)ᵏ‖ ≥ 0.5803ᵏ`.  The mechanism consumes S-unit/radical integer structure
   (`3ᵏ − 2ᵏq`), and **dies for transcendental constants** — a 2026 survey calls `‖ξαⁿ‖` for
   (1, e) "completely out of reach."

## Verdict, priced for our graph

The sweep's closing analysis lands directly on our architecture: **abc as currently harnessed
gives infinitude, never density**, and every candidate path to "abc ⇒ digits of ln 2" is
missing the same *almost-all upgrade*:

- **Path A (our R3 door)**: needs (i) `log x` → density-one non-Wieferich (unproven even under
  explicit abc) AND (ii) vanishing-control → prescribed-nonzero-residue control (no

- **Path B (S-unit route)**: abc genuinely floors `‖ξ(a/b)ᵏ‖` because the event is an integer

  converts the digit event into an S-unit-shaped one*.  Our `q_p(2)` coincidence certificate
  IS such a conversion, at the single position `p − 1`.  What the literature lacks is any
  theorem making that event rare for almost all `p`.
