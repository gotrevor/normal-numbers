## The must-cite frontier papers

- **Rivoal 2007**, *Convergents and irrationality measures of logarithms*, Rev. Mat. Iberoam. 23,
  931–952 ([PDF](https://rivoal.perso.math.cnrs.fr/articles/logconvergents.pdf)).  **Nearest prior
  art.**  Restricted irrationality measures against denominators `d_m·B^m`, `d_m = lcm(1..m)` —
  exactly our denominator class, explicitly motivated by binary digits of logs — via asymmetric
  Padé approximants.  For ln 2: exponent 4.622 (pure `2^m`), 1.948967 (class `d_m 2^m`, Thm 4(ii));
  sweep-agent arithmetic converts these to run bounds ≈ 3.62n / 3.76n — *weaker than
  Marcovecchio's 2.57n*.  His strong digit conclusions apply only to log(1±a/b), b large, not
  ln 2.  ⚠️ Owed: a forward-citation pass on this paper (the residual hole in the negative).
- **Rivoal 2008**, *On the bits counting function of real numbers*, J. Austral. Math. Soc.
  ([PDF](https://rivoal.perso.math.cnrs.fr/articles/binreal.pdf)).  `B_n(x) ≥ log n / log(μ+ε)`;
  §7 introduces the restricted exponent `ν_{x,2}` as the right quantity; remarks Diophantine
  methods "probably not strong enough to prove normality".
- **Bugeaud–Kim, arXiv:2510.02059**: subword complexity from irrationality exponents, nontrivial
  only for μ < 2.324 — **inapplicable to ln 2** (paper itself lists μ(log 2) as not known < that).
- **Lagarias 2001**, *On the normality of arithmetical constants*, Exp. Math. 10
  (arXiv:math/0101055, read in full; local copy fetched).  The acid, precisely: Thm 3.1 + Lemma
  3.2 show the surrogate orbit shadows `bⁿθ mod 1` (same limit points, u.d. ⟺ u.d.), so
  **Hypothesis A for θ is exactly equivalent to "θ rational or b-normal"** — hypothesis and
  conclusion are one statement in two coordinate systems.  Remark 1 after Thm 3.3: *every* real
  has a perturbed-b-expansion of this shape, so the dynamical mechanism alone is contentless; all
  content must live in the arithmetic of the kicks.  🚨 **Constraint on our (c)**: his adversarial
  family survives a magnitude floor `ε_n ≥ c/n`, so a kick-floor-only abstract lemma can NEVER
  conclude u.d.-or-finite — conclusions must stay at the forcing/structure level ("run ⇒ ride"),
  which ours do.  Check any generalized statement against this family before claiming it.
  💡 His footnote-1 open problem — density-one agreement of true vs surrogate digits — is a
  pointwise question our kick machinery plausibly touches; candidate extra deliverable.
- **Bailey–Borwein 2012**, *Nonnormality of Stoneham constants*, Ramanujan J. 29: the orbit
  provably rides exponentially close to 0 ⇒ long zero-runs — the easy converse direction of a
  bottom sliver, for an engineered constant with exactly computable kick.  Compare-against.
- **Marcovecchio 2009**, *The Rhin–Viola method for log 2*, Acta Arith. 139, μ ≤ 3.574553…
  (best unrestricted; checked Dec-2025 arXiv:2512.12890 — bounds nonquadraticity, not μ).

## The wall, located exactly (harmonic-numerator sweep)

- Our orbit IS Bailey–Crandall 2001 eq. (2) verbatim (iterate list matches `Aₙ/Dₙ`).  The
  coincidence question is a **pointwise refinement of the known-open Hypothesis A instance for
  ln 2**; Lagarias §6 places the family beside Furstenberg ×2×3 rigidity.  Wall-class: confirmed.
- **Why Stoneham falls and ln 2 stands**: same dynamical scheme; Stoneham's perturbation
  denominators are *smooth*, ours contain every prime ≤ n.  The obstruction is the large primes
  in lcm(1..n).
- **Measure arguments structurally cannot reach the window**: they exclude widths ≲ q^{2−μ}
  integers at scale q = 2ⁿDₙ; the coincidence window has width < 1 integer, i.e. scale q⁻¹.
  Even μ → 2 leaves constant-width windows untouchable.  (Borel–Cantelli gives avoidance for
  a.e. real — metrically trivial, individually hopeless, the signature of the class.)
- 💎 **Fermat-quotient bridge** (usable arithmetic): at `n = p−1`,
  `A_{p−1} ≡ unit·q_p(2) (mod p)` where `q_p(2)` is the Fermat quotient — via Glaisher
  (`Σ_{k<p} 2ᵏ/k ≡ −2q_p(2) mod p`) and Z.-H. Sun
  (`Σ_{k<p} 1/(k·2ᵏ) ≡ q_p(2) − (p/2)q_p(2)² + … mod p³`).  So window-avoidance mod p at
  prime-adjacent indices reduces to non-Wieferich-type conditions (themselves open;
  infinitude of non-Wieferich primes known only under abc, Silverman 1988).  Handle for the
  p-adic door (alien R3) and for probe design.
- Distribution of harmonic-type numerators mod p: **no theorem exists** (Boyd's random-walk
  model is heuristic; Eswarathasan–Levine finiteness open for every prime; best counts
  Sanna 2016 / Wu–Chen, density 0).  Numerators mod lcm: untouched in the literature.
- Elementary live facts: for each prime `p ∈ (n/2, n]`, the term `2^{n−p}/p` gives
  `ν_p(xₙ) = −1` — so every such p divides Dₙ exactly, and `xₙ ≠ 0`.  The 2-adic
  Kürschák-style trick is dead here (`Σ 2ʲ/j = −log(−1) = 0` in ℚ₂).
