## The idea

Center each prime's phase before expanding the frozen product `Φ(n) = ∏_p ψ_p(n)`:
`ψ_p = μ_p + φ_p`, with `μ_p = E ψ_p = 1 + a_p/p` and `a_p = Σ_{j: p ≤ y_j} w_j`.  Then
`Φ = Σ_S c_S`, with `c_S = ∏_{p∉S} μ_p ∏_{p∈S} φ_p` of exact conductor `d_S = ∏_{p∈S} p`.

## Model side: checks out (paper, 2026-10-10)

- Since `|ψ_p| = 1`, the energies `E_S = ‖c_S‖²` form a product measure: `p ∈ S` independently
  with probability `Var_p = (1/p) Σ_{j: p≤y_j} |w_j|² − |a_p|²/p²`.  So `Σ_S E_S = 1`.
- `Σ_S E_S log d_S = Σ_p Var_p log p ≈ δ (log N/u²) Σ_j |w_j|² 2^{−j}`.  This is J-free (the
  `2^{−j}` comes from `log y_j`), and `Σ_j |w_j|² 2^{−j} ≤ 4`.
- By Markov, the energy above `N^θ` is `≪ δ/(u²θ)`.
- The ℓ¹ weight of a centered factor is `≈ W/p` with `W = Σ_j |w_j| = O(log|h|)`.  So the BV step
  carries `W^{ω(d)}` times a fixed log power, and Cauchy–Schwarz against the trivial bound absorbs it
  into `Literature.GranvilleShaoCor71` / `MultBVResidue`.  The uncentered expansion had the same
  ℓ¹ weights but no level control; centering supplies the level control.  (Astra's coefficient
  ledger in mail `20261010T231117Z-astra-…` agrees.)

## Exact-in-T formula (one parity site, `z = −1`)

`M·B_d` is the law of a random squarefree `D′`, each P-prime `≤ y` included with probability `2/p`.  Then

    Φ_≤D(T) − Φ(T) = − Σ_{e⊆T} c_T(e) τ_{¬T}(log D − log e),
    c_T(e) = ∏_{p∈e} 2(1−p)/p · ∏_{p∈T∖e} (1 − 2/p),

- `T` is the set of P-primes dividing `m`.
- `τ_{¬T}` is the tail of `Σ X_p log p`, with `X_p ~ Bern(2/p)` over `p ∉ T`.
- Per prime of `T`, this is the operator `(1−2/p) I + 2(1−p)/p · shift_{log p}`.  It is `−1` on
  constants, so only the variation of `τ` on scale `log y` survives.

## Heuristic: the y → ∞ limit has all moments finite: WITHDRAWN 2026-10-11 (see Resume)

- Put `v = log p / log y`.  Points of `T` form a Poisson process with intensity `δ dv/v`.
- `τ` tends to a generalized-Dickman(2δ) tail, which is Hölder-`2δ` at 0 and smooth beyond.
- Tiny points contribute factors `1 + O(v^{2δ})`, which are summable against `dv/v`.
- Each mid-size point contributes a factor ≤ 3, and `P(m mid-size points) ~ (δ log m)^m/m!`.
- So `P(|Φ_≤| > t)` decays faster than every power of `t`.
- Finite-y effects (the `D′ = 1` atom of mass `≈ (log y)^{−2δ}`, prime lattice) should fade slowly.
- With complex phases, the per-point factor becomes about `1 + 2W`, so `M4 ≲ exp(O(δ W⁴))`.
  In the Erdős–Turán chain, `W ≍ log(1/ρ)` and `u = ρ^{−2}`, so `e^{−u/2}` absorbs it.

### Finite-y proof of uniform M4 for the Riesz cutoff (Astra, refereed by Opus 2026-10-11)

Source: `agent-mail/nn-coefmass/20261011T124359Z-astra-…`.  This **supersedes the finite-y step of
the sketch above.**  Astra's objection to it stands: at fixed y, simultaneous prime-phase
resonances, e.g. a local factor `1 + 64/p` at r = −1 instead of the angular `1 + 24/p`, block a
pointwise high-frequency bound from the circle average.  The limit-model criterion k > 6δ remains
sufficient there, not necessary.

- **(L) Local lemma.**  Take ψ of unit modulus, `μ = Eψ`, `φ = ψ − μ` and `V = 1 − |μ|²`.  For
  `|r| ≤ 1` and `q ≥ |1−r|`:  `E|μ + rφ|⁴ ≤ 1 + 18 V min(1, q²)`.
  - Constant case: the expansion terms are bounded by `|μ|⁴ + (2+4+8+4)V`.
  - Small-q case: write `μ + rφ = ψ − uφ` with `Re u ≥ 0`, and use `E(φψ̄) = V` (real), which gives
    `1 − 4V Re u + (6|u|²+8|u|³+4|u|⁴)V`.
- **(P)** `H_y(s) = ∏_p (μ_p + e^{−s v_p} φ_p)` exactly.  The primes are independent, `|r_p| ≤ 1`
  and `|1−r_p| ≤ |s| v_p`, so `E|H_y|⁴ ≤ exp(18 Σ_p V_p min(1, |s|² v_p²))`.
- **(B) Untwisted Mertens.**  `Σ_{p≤y} min(1, (R log p/log y)²)/p ≤ log R + C₀`, split at
  `z = y^{1/R}`.  With `V_p ≤ A/p` and `A = Σ_j |w_j|²`:
  `E|H_y(s)|⁴ ≤ e^{18 C₀ A} max(1,|s|)^{18A}`, uniformly in y, J and Re s ≥ 0.
- **(R) Riesz inversion.**  `P_k(K) = k! K^{−k} (2π)^{−1} ∫ e^{sK} H(s) s^{−k−1} dt` at `σ = 1/K`.
  Minkowski gives, for integer `k > β = 9A/2` and K ≥ 1,
  `‖P_k(K)‖₄ ≤ (k! e/2π) e^{9C₀A/2} [2^{β/2} π + 2/(k−β)]`, uniformly in y, K and J.
  - Parity site: A = 4, so k = 19 works universally (k = 10 using δ = 1/2).
  - ET phases: A ≤ 2W, so k > 9W suffices, and `log M4 = O(k log k + A)`.  Since W = O(log 1/ρ),
    `u = ρ^{−2}` absorbs it.
- **Energy and support.**  `(1 − (1−x)_+^k)² ≤ kx`, so the energy cutoff costs a factor k.
  Support stays `d ≤ D` and the weights stay ≤ 1.
