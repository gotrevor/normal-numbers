## 0. What the second story taught first: two collapses and a refutation 🧯

Building on sand still finds the load-bearing rock, by collapsing everything that isn't.

**(a) The Fermat-quotient coordinate is cosmetic at every scale.**  For a candidate digit
path `s̃`, the mod-p checksum test "does `q_p(2)` match the path's forced value" algebraically
reduces to `s̃ ≡ s (mod p)` — agreement with the TRUE digits — because the bridge identity
`q_p ≡ s + res·L⁻¹` holds identically and `q_p` cancels.  Sharper: the whole checksum
identity at `n = p−1` contains exactly **one bit** of digit information (the wrap/sliver
indicator `⌊2^{p−1}·ln 2⌋ − ⌊A/L⌋ ∈ {0,1}`); everything else is computable.  Consequences:
no external Fermat-quotient theorem (abc/Silverman non-Wieferich included) can feed the ln-2
tower; the imagined rung "disjunctivity of ln 2 from FQ-equidistribution" is the conclusion
in a costume (Lagarias acid, again); N1 survives *only* as what the slate honestly called it,
a weakening-lattice digit hypothesis wearing arithmetic coordinates.
**Map-level payoff:** the genuinely external doors into this problem family are exactly
three — Diophantine/archimedean (measures, Baker), dynamical (rigidity/effectivity), and
internal counting with provable equidistribution seeds.  The mod-p door is intrinsically
self-referential.

**(b) T4 (the e kick-barrier) — probe-REFUTED same session.** 🔬  The tower's intended crown
was: runs of e must survive factorial-threshold kicks; surviving kick `k` at depth `d`
requires `dist(frac(log₂ k!), ℤ) < 2^{−d}`; `{log₂ k!}` is equidistributed (classical, van
der Corput class) with effective discrepancy ⟹ an *unconditional counting theorem* for long
runs of e.  The probe (200k bits): kicks crossed by the 20 super-threshold runs have
population-generic distances (median `~2^{−2}`, e.g. the 18-bit record crosses `k=3118` at
`2^{−1.2}`), and run ends do not hug kicks (normalized gap 0.65 vs uniform 0.5).  **Dead.**
Mechanism error, identified: the crossing term transfers an *integer* between bookkeeping
columns, and integers are invisible on the circle — the true orbit is pure doubling, the
ride is the same smooth unstable-profile tracking as ln 2's, no barrier.  N3's actual
content (dichotomy, τ-floor, rigid numerators, all-base) is untouched; only the barrier
super-structure falls.

## 1. T3 — the solenoid/Weyl door (now the top of the tower) 🚪

**Rung `ShortOrbitCancel h`:** for each fixed `h`, the Weyl sum
`Σ_j e(h·v_j)` over block `k`'s nondegenerate readout segment is `o(A_k)` along infinitely
many blocks.  **Edges:** `ShortOrbitCancel` for `h ≤ 6^ℓ` ⟹ (Erdős–Turán, elementary,
lane-2-formalizable) block visits to every length-ℓ cylinder ⟹ `PowersOfThreeReadoutDense`
⟹ (A2) `α₂,₃` is 6-disjunctive.

**Probe (blocks 5–11, h = 1..8):** cancellation at square-root strength — `|S_h|/A` tracks
`A^{−1/2}` within small factors at every `h` (block 11: predicted 0.0030, observed
0.0012–0.0055); length-3 word coverage 216/216 from block 7 on.  The orbit segments are
Weyl-random to the eye at exactly the random-model rate.

**The wall, located exactly:** full-period equidistribution of `g^a/2^c` is classical
(Postnikov–Korobov); Bourgain–Glibichuk–Konyagin-style bounds reach segments of length
`q^δ`, `q = 2^c`; our segments have length `Θ(c) = Θ(log q)` — **sub-polynomial, below every
known technique**.  So the rung is honest sand, but it sits in a named active theory
(exponential sums over multiplicative subgroups + solenoid rigidity), the probe shows the
truth holds with room to spare, and A5/BLMV is the effectivity school nearest the gap.
Softening worth naming: disjunctivity needs only *infinitely many good blocks*, i.e. one
visit per word per ∞-many k — far weaker than per-block equidistribution; no known technique
exploits the union, which makes it a genuinely open direction rather than a graveyard.

## 2. T3c — **PROVABLE BRICK**: the staircase slices are the two-exponential family 💎

Not sand.  Found while building: fix a slope through the staircase.  At readout coordinates
`(a, c)` with `a = γc`:

- **Mid-block (`γ = 1`, position `n = (3^k + k)/2`):** the readout is literally
  `frac((3/2)^m)`, `m = (3^k − k)/2`.  **The base-6 digits of α₂,₃ sample Mahler's
  `(3/2)^n` problem at mid-block positions.**
- **Critical slope (`γ* = log 2/log 3 ≈ 0.631`, one explicit `n` per block):** the readout
  approaches 1 exactly when `|a·log₂3 − c|` is small — i.e. the two-log separation quantity
  `‖m·log 2 − k·log 3‖` that collatz-moonshot's `sep_two_three` / Legendre engine BOUNDS,
  unconditionally, with formalized machinery already vendored into this repo (D14).

**Theorem candidate (one wiring lap, no new mathematics needed):** via the readout theorem's
exp-small error + the proved two-log separation, **unconditional caps on base-6 digit runs
of α₂,₃ at the explicit slice positions** — the first statement anywhere in the program
where the collatz repo's Diophantine engine outputs a digit theorem about a normal-numbers
constant.  Small, but real, and exactly the "pair a new rung with a dischargeable node"
ratio the doctrine demands.  Odds provable in one lap: ~80%.  This also *upgrades the N2
bridge claim*: the shared wall is now object-shared in both directions with a worked example
(their separation ⟹ our digit caps; our readout density ⟺ their orbit density).

## 3. T1 — the Poisson layer above N1 📊

**Rung `PairMiss`:** at most one deep-approach event per dyadic window (equivalently:
coincidences at `(n, p)` and `(n′, p′)` in one window never co-occur beyond the random
rate — they are coupled through the `A`-recurrence, so a double event is a single
larger-modulus coincidence).  Census evidence: 5 coincidences, all in distinct windows.
**Edges:** PairMiss + N1 ⟹ counting laws on runs (`#{n ≤ N : run > log₂ n + t} ≲ 2^{−t}·ln N`)

ladder from the arithmetic side.  Currency: internal digit-hypothesis (per §0a there is no
external feed); legitimate as a strict weakening with checkable failure events.

## 4. T4′ — what remains of the e-door: one-sided derandomization 🌗

After the barrier refutation, the honest residue: e's exclusion family is the cleanest-stated
in the program because its arithmetic side is *rigid* — `A(M) mod p = A(M mod p)` makes the
forced-value sequence provably periodic/analyzable, so the e-coincidence conjectures have the
form "an explicitly periodic sequence avoids the digit-window sequence."  One side of every
coincidence is now a theorem-side, not a heuristic-side.  The digit side remains the wall,
as §0a says it must.  Value: sharpest testbed for exclusion-node *statements* (any future
technique that can consume rigid mod-p structure meets its cleanest instance here).
