# The base-3 wing: single-digit disjunctions 🥔

2026-08-29, seventh story.  Probes: `experiments/base3_digit_hunt.py`,
`experiments/base3_cantor_beam.py`.  The adder machine is base-agnostic (digit = v mod 3,
carry = v div 3, carry range [a⁻+b⁻, a⁺+b⁺], same superset-soundness argument as the
mixed-sign base-2 note); what changes is the *frontier*.

## Why base 3 is the sharpest wing: the frontier drops to length ONE 🎯

In base 2, words 0, 1, 01, 10 recur for every irrational and the open frontier starts at
{00, 11}.  In base 3 an irrational can avoid a single digit forever (Cantor middle
thirds: uncountably many irrationals with no digit 1), so **"digit d occurs i.o." is
open for every specific constant and every digit** - the shortest possible open words.
Avoid-one-digit automata have ONE live word-state, so certificates shrink drastically.

## Theorem candidate: a four-channel SINGLE-DIGIT collapse, 72 live states 💎

General greedy (channels free to pick their digit) collapsed in four steps, exact
(integer graph, 72 live states, all fixed points):

> **For any reals X, Y not both rational, at least one holds in ternary:**
> - **digit 0 occurs i.o. in Y** (instance: ln 3)
> - **digit 2 occurs i.o. in 3Y** (instance: ln 27)
> - **digit 0 occurs i.o. in 3X + Y** (instance: ln 24)
> - **digit 2 occurs i.o. in X + Y** (instance: ln 6)

Four channels, single digits, 72 states - versus six channels and 9 478 states for the
cheapest base-2 theorem (musical).  Base 3 is where this genre's certificates are
smallest and the statements shortest.  The base-3 complement involution (d → 2−d,
self-test-verified to 4 decimals) gives the twin (digit 2 in ln 3, digit 0 in ln 27,
digit 2 in ln 24, digit 0 in ln 6) free.

## The escape-from-Cantor hunt 🏜️

Headline target: every channel avoids **digit 1** - no channel family can be jointly
middle-thirds-like.  Greedy stalled at h = 0.0068 after 10 channels, but the beam over
channel *sets* (`base3_cantor_beam.py` - the musical lesson applied: greedy floors are
myopia) **closed it at FIVE channels, exact, 261 live states, all fixed points**:

> **For any reals X, Y not both rational, at least one of Y, 2Y, X+4Y, 2X, 4X contains
> ternary digit 1 infinitely often.**
> Instance (ln 2, ln 3): one of **ln 3, ln 9, ln 162, ln 4, ln 16** has digit 1 i.o. in
> ternary - these five constants cannot all be Cantor-like.
> Instance (Y = X, any irrational x): **at least one of x, 2x, 4x, 5x contains ternary
> digit 1 infinitely often** - a four-multiple, coefficient-≤5, single-digit statement.

⚠️ The Y = X instance sits closest to Mahler/Berend-Boshernitzan territory of anything
the factory has produced (their genre: for irrational α, SOME multiple m ≤ 2g^(k+1)
realizes a chosen word; for g=3, k=1 that allows m up to 18).  Differences to verify in
the novelty sweep before any claim: their word is adversary-chosen and ours is fixed
(digit 1); our multiple set {1,2,4,5} is explicit and far below 18; whether their
occurrence is i.o.; and our two-real universal form has no analogue there.  The
novelty risk is HIGHEST on this instance - check B-B Acta Arith 66 exactly.
Digit 1 is self-complementary, so the tuple is its own involution twin; greedy's
10-channel 0.0068 floor was myopia once again.

- ⚠️ **New code path**: Channel3/entropy3/exact_zero3 are a fresh implementation, not
  the battle-tested base-2 engine.  Self-tests: h(full) = log2 9, Cantor track log2 6,

  itself - the independent-reimplementation debt is *higher* here than in the base-2
  wing, and the 72-state certificate is small enough to hand-verify (worth doing before
  any outward whisper).
- Soundness of the carry range mirrors the family-enum superset note; re-derived for
  base 3 (T(n) = a{3ⁿX} + b{3ⁿY} − {3ⁿz} ∈ [a⁻+b⁻, a⁺+b⁺]), not independently checked.
- Novelty sweep owed: single-digit-omission literature (digit-avoiding irrationals,
  Cantor-set arithmetic - Hochman-Shmerkin / Wu on ×2 ×3 invariant measures are the
  neighborhood; is a statement like "one of finitely many linear forms must realize a
  given ternary digit" known?).  The universality framing (any pair not both rational)
  still applies; cite Mahler 1973 / Berend-Boshernitzan as genre ancestors.
- Vacuity: single ternary digits are genuinely open words (no analogue of the base-2
  known-recurring boundary below length 2), so no channel is carrying a vacuous word.
