## 🧠 Ideas worth carrying

1. **The route's two halves are ONE hypothesis.**  "The pullback needs a width floor" and "the
   counting needs bounded bursts" are the same statement (S7-BL).  Stop treating them separately.
2. **The crux's residual is now localized**: the *frequency* of narrow states (= of long bursts),
   plus the *distribution of the basepoints* `O_t⁻¹ Φ (p_n/q_n)` that long bursts spell out.  No
   pullback estimate can touch a forced block — that is a theorem now, not a worry.
3. **CF-normality does NOT give uniform integrability of `log a`.**  A CF-normal `x` can have
   `(1/N)Σ log aₙ → ∞` (overwrite a CF-normal sequence's digits at positions `k!` with `2^{k!}`;
   density-0 perturbations do not move word frequencies).  So the burst frequency need not tend
   to 0, and for such `x` the §7 question genuinely reduces to the CF statistics of `φ·p/q`.
   Candidate witness for a refutation lap; not yet Lean (needs a digit-sequence → real map,
   which the repo does not have).
4. **Gauss invariance is the reason the block estimate is lossless.**  `γ(G^{-j}I_w) = γ(I_w)`
   is what makes `blockPullback_sum_le` linear in `L` instead of accumulating distortion.
