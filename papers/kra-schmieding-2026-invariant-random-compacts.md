## Relevance verdict

The paper develops invariant random compacts and proves multiplicative-largeness
results for dilations of positive-entropy invariant compact sets on the circle.  Its
Corollary 1.4 says the multipliers making such a set epsilon-dense form a
multiplicatively syndetic set.

This requires an invariant compact set with positive entropy and lets the successful
multiplier depend on that set and on epsilon.  It neither supplies a fixed finite set
for every irrational orbit nor treats digit avoidance in fixed two-real linear forms.
