## Relevance verdict

This sequel proves Glasner properties for epimorphic actions on homogeneous spaces
and for broad linear groups acting on tori.  It is a direct higher-dimensional
dynamics descendant of the Alon-Peres neighborhood.

Again the acting element is existential in an infinite group.  The paper does not
address a prescribed finite list of multipliers or recurring base digits.
