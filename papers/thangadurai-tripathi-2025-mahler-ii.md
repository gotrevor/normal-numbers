## Relevance verdict

Theorem 1 assumes that `alpha` contains a long zero block followed by a
specified nonzero block `C`.  Subject to an explicit inequality involving
the target block `B` and `C`, every integer in a displayed interval makes
`B` appear at the corresponding position.  Corollary 2 converts repeated
source blocks of frequency `nu` into target-block frequency at least `nu`.

This is an explicit-interval refinement under digit-pattern hypotheses on
`alpha`.  It does not yield an unconditional universal fixed finite set and
does not subsume any two-track tower claim.
