## Relevance verdict

This is a direct Mahler 1973 citer and the closest later analogue by theorem shape.
Its Theorem 1.2 proves that, for an irrational Laurent series over a function field,
some polynomial multiplier `X` makes every length-`N` digit word occur infinitely
often.  The proof gives the bound `|X| < |G|^(2N+1)`, independent of the source
irrational.

The multiplier is still selected from a bounded family according to the irrational.
The setting is a positive-characteristic function field, and the paper neither gives
a fixed two-element real multiplier set nor treats the two-real linear-form problem.
