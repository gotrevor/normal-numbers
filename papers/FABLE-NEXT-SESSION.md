## Lane B: G4, genuinely new cancellation

Use EventualPrefixDecay or the schedule-only WindowDecayK, not PrefixDecay.
The first concrete question is whether the geometric coefficients allow a
shifted-correlation estimate unavailable for arbitrary Elliott correlations.
One-site Halasz does NOT apply to a product at distinct shifts merely
because each factor separately has small mean.

A required stress test: at primes the product of the individual functions is
exp(2*pi*i*h*(1-4^(-k))/3).  When 3 divides h, this tends to 1 as k grows.
Its distance squared from 1 is O_h(16^(-k)*sum_{p<=M}1/p).
At k=windowK(M), the prime harmonic sum is O(loglog M) and 16^k is
comparable to (loglog M)^4, so this diagnostic tends to zero, not infinity.
This is an elementary diagnostic calculation to verify independently,
not a claimed obstruction to the shifted correlation itself.  A
product-nonpretentiousness shortcut cannot simply be made uniform here.

Require any candidate estimate to specify:
1. ordinary rather than logarithmic averaging;
2. every sufficiently large scale rather than almost-all scales;
3. a bound with its dependence on k and h, evaluated at windowK(M);
4. treatment of resonant frequencies (4, 3, 12), not just h=1;
5. a genuinely new analytic input, not renamed rough-part independence.

DESIGN-2026-09-19-bcr-wiring.md before proposing machinery.  As primary-source
