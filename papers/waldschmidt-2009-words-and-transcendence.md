transcendence in digit expansions.  It cites Mahler and
Berend-Boshernitzan 1994 as part of this genre.  It contains no fixed finite
multiplier set matching C2 and no theorem found to subsume the two-real
linear-form disjunctions.  Its role in the audit is literature orientation,
