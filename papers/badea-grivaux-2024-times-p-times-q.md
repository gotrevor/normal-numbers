## Relevance verdict

The paper disproves a measure-theoretic Furstenberg conjecture by constructing
generic invariant measures with large Fourier coefficients.  It mentions Alon-Peres
only while surveying Glasner sets and notes that a finite union of lacunary sequences
is not a Glasner set.

That negative result concerns arbitrary fine density for every infinite compact set.
It does not rule out the tower's fixed, length-one digit hitting problem, and the paper
contains no theorem of the C2 or two-track form.
