## Relevance verdict

For a prescribed lacunary sequence `(a_n)`, this paper estimates the largest gap in
`{a_1 x,...,a_N x}` for Lebesgue-almost every `x`.  It cites Alon-Peres as related
uniform-dilation background.

Its result is metric rather than universal, concerns growing lacunary families, and
allows an exceptional null set of inputs.  It does not imply any tower claim.
