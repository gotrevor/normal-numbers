## Relevance verdict

Rajchert extends Nair-Velani and Bulinski-Fish to maps between tori whose matrix
entries are polynomial values at primes.  The main theorem is a quantitative Glasner
property for this infinite class of matrices.

The matrix entries are variables selected to obtain density.  Nothing in the result
reduces them to a fixed finite matrix family, and the conclusion is not a theorem about
digit recurrence.  It does not threaten the tower novelty claims.
