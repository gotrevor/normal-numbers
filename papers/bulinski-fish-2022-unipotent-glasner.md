## Relevance verdict

The paper classifies polynomial integer matrices having the Glasner property on the
full torus and proves a uniform Glasner theorem for suitable unipotently generated
groups.  It cites Alon-Peres as the quantitative framework.

As in the rest of the Glasner branch, a matrix or group element is chosen from an
infinite action after seeing the input set.  There is no universal finite multiplier
pair and no fixed digit-recurrence disjunction.
