## Relevance verdict

The paper proves Weyl-type equidistribution in function fields and applies it to van
der Corput, intersective, and Glasner sets.  Its Glasner theorem is a
positive-characteristic analogue with a polynomial parameter selected from an
infinite family.

It is not a theorem about real base expansions, and it gives neither a universal
two-element multiplier set nor a fixed family of two-real linear forms.
