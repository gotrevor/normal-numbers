## Relevance verdict

This direct Alon-Peres descendant proves quantitative Glasner properties for
irreducible linear group actions and certain product actions.  Its product theorem is
the most plausible citation-cone neighbor of the tower's two-track statements.

The acting group element is nevertheless chosen after seeing the set, and the
conclusion is density of an image in a torus.  It does not select among a prescribed
finite list of linear forms and does not force recurrence of a prescribed digit in one
of those forms.  It does not subsume the flagship, C6, C7, or C10.
