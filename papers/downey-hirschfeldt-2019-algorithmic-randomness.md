## Why it is here

The readable on-ramp to the effective-randomness ladder (Kurtz < Schnorr < computably random <
Martin-Löf < 2-random) that `docs/irregularity-landscape.md` and
`docs/how-irregular-is-a-number.html` place above this repo's subject.  Covers the three
equivalent definitions of Martin-Löf randomness (effective null sets, incompressibility,
c.e. martingales), which is the background for "Martin-Löf random ⇒ absolutely normal" and for
Chaitin's Ω sitting at Martin-Löf in the lattice.  Expository survey by the authors of the
standard textbook (*Algorithmic Randomness and Complexity*, Springer 2010).
