## Results used in the tower audit

Corollary 7.2 gives a short Furstenberg/Glasner proof that for every base
`b>1` and irrational `alpha`, some integer `n` has every base-`b` digit
occurring infinitely often in `n alpha`.

The following remark says more: the good multipliers have density one, and the
multiplier can be restricted to a prescribed Glasner set, including squares and
primes.  This makes the qualitative phenomenon behind C2 classical and robust.
It does not provide a fixed finite hitting set that works for every irrational.
