## Relevance verdict

This is the only substantive direct citer of Berend-Boshernitzan 1995 found by
OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, and COCI.  It proves density modulo one for infinite
families such as

    {(p1^n q1^m + p2^n q2^m) alpha : n,m in N}

for every irrational `alpha`, under multiplicative-independence hypotheses.  This is
a Furstenberg-semigroup generalization, not a bounded universal multiplier theorem.
It has no fixed two-element family and no simultaneous digit-avoidance conclusion.
