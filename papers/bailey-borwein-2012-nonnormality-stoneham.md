# Bailey–Borwein 2012 — pin note (read 2026-10-05, abstract and §3)

D. H. Bailey, J. M. Borwein, *Nonnormality of Stoneham constants*, Ramanujan J. 29 (2012) 409-422,
doi:10.1007/s11139-012-9417-3.  PDF alongside (gitignored), from `carmamaths.org/jon/abnormality.pdf`.

- Abstract: `α_{b,c}` is not `bc`-normal, so `α_{2,3}` is provably not 6-normal; extended to
  `B = b^p c^q r` when `D = c^(q/p) r^(1/p) / b^(c−1) < 1`.
- §3 sketches the `α_{2,3}` base-6 case (first shown in their ref. [5]): an excess of long zero runs.
