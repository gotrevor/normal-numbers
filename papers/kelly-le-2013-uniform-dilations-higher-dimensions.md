## Relevance verdict

This is the strongest direct higher-dimensional descendant of Alon-Peres in the
forward-citation cone.  For polynomial integer matrices `A(n)` acting between tori,
it gives conditions under which some integer `n` makes `A(n)X` dense in an appropriate
subtorus, together with an effective finite-set version.

The quantifier over `n` remains existential and depends on `X` and the requested
density.  The paper does not provide a universal fixed finite set of multipliers, a
base-expansion recurrence theorem, or a fixed disjunction of two-real linear forms.
It therefore does not subsume C2 or the tower's genuine two-track claims.
