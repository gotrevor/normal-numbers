## Relevance verdict

The paper proves quantitative occurrence-frequency variants under a hypothesis
that a suitable zero block followed by a nonzero digit occurs in `alpha` with
a frequency.  It then produces a multiplier for a prescribed target block with
a corresponding positive frequency.

The extra hypothesis is essential to the paper's conclusions.  It gives neither
an unconditional fixed pair such as `{2,11}` nor a simultaneous theorem for
linear forms in two arbitrary reals.
