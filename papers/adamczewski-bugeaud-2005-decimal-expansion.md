## Relevance verdict

This is an expository paper on combinatorial complexity and transcendence of
algebraic irrational digit expansions.  Near the end it records Mahler's
existence theorem and cites Berend-Boshernitzan 1994.  It does not analyze
universal fixed finite multiplier sets or simultaneous linear forms.

Semantic Scholar, but not OpenAlex, placed it in the direct citation cone of
Berend-Boshernitzan 1994.  It was read to close that index delta; it does not
change the tower novelty classification.
