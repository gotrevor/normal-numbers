# Becher–Heiber–Slaman 2015 — pin note (read 2026-10-05, §1)

V. Becher, P. A. Heiber, T. A. Slaman, *A computable absolutely normal Liouville number*,
Math. Comp. 84 (2015) 2939-2952.  PDF alongside (gitignored), from Becher's page.
