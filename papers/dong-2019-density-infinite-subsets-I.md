## Relevance verdict

Dong proves that the natural `SL(n,Z)` action on the `n`-torus has the Glasner
property and studies quantitative density under other dynamics.  Alon-Peres is
background for the one-dimensional quantitative problem.

The theorem chooses an element of an infinite matrix group after seeing the set.  It
has no fixed finite multiplier family or digit-expansion conclusion.
