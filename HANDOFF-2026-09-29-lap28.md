## 🧠 The two ideas worth carrying

1. **Ergodicity was nearly free from what the repo already had.**  `gaussMeasure_cylinder_mixing`
   bounds the correlation against an ARBITRARY measurable set, geometrically in the gap `g`.  For
   an invariant set the left-hand side does not depend on `g`, so the error is forced to **zero**:
   `γ(I_v ∩ A) = γ(I_v)γ(A)` exactly, no approximation.  Cylinders → opens needed only exact
   monotonicity of the depth-`n` cylinder bodies, which is bought by restricting to the
   irrationals (rationals are γ-null), plus the `2/2^n` diameter decay.
2. **Krylov–Bogolyubov needs neither Prokhorov nor portmanteau here.**  The hypothesis is a
   uniform ABSOLUTE CONTINUITY bound, so the ultrafilter limit of the empirical CDFs is
   **Lipschitz**, not merely monotone.  Continuity by construction kills every continuity-point
   caveat: the limit is a `StieltjesFunction`, and `stieltjes_measure_le` (compare INCREMENTS;
   both outer measures are infima over the same `Ioc` covers) gives `ν ≤ C • volume` without any
   dyadic/Whitney decomposition of open sets.  And the Gauss map's discontinuities are handled by
   the EXPLICIT branch decomposition `mem_branch_iff`, not by a mapping theorem — irrationality
   of the orbit points removes the single boundary case `x = 1/(k+1)`.
