why the brief's §7.1 says `b ≥ 3` and not `b ≥ 2`.  At `b = 3` the mass is `(2/3)^K/2 = e^{−Θ(K)}`
against `εη = e^{−Θ(√K)}/K`, so it closes.  Full `b`-table for all five `ScheduleWitness`
inequalities in `PENDING_WORK.md` §G4B.  A repair (the stretch target, NOT the objective) would
