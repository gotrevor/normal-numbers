## 🧠 The seven ideas worth carrying

1. **Legendre, elementary** (`exists_eq_cfK_of_good`, `T ≥ 24`).  With `m = round(qy)` and
   `c p := q·pₚ − m·qₚ`, either `c p = 0` (⟹ `q = qₚ`, both fractions primitive) or `|c p| ≥ 1`,
   and expanding `−c p = q(qₚy − pₚ) − qₚ(qy − m)` gives the **self-propagating** bound
   `1 ≤ 2q/qₚ₊₁ + 2qₚ/(Tq)`: `qₚ ≤ 3q ⟹ qₚ₊₁ ≤ 8q/3 ≤ 3q`, contradicting `qₚ → ∞`.  No sign
   alternation of `qₙy − pₙ` (which the repo lacks), no Fibonacci growth.
2. **The Diophantine detour is closed as a detour.**  Lap 39: the two counts bound each other.
   Lap 40: `constCF T = (√(T²+4) − T)/2` (Gauss-map fixed point, all digits `T`) makes EVERY
   convergent denominator good, so `GoodDenBoundPrim` is false whenever `D log(T+1) < T` — lap
   34's free rate is ATTAINED and the wall is real.  Laps 31–40 bought a reformulation, not a
   reduction.
3. **The bootstrap fails.**  `blockCount_cellSet_le_shift` gives `freq(w,T) ≤ freq([],T) + |w|`
   unconditionally, but the crux needs the PRODUCT `γ(I_w)·γ(cellSet [] T)`, and
   `not_min_le_const_mul` kills `min a b ≤ C·a·b` for every `C`.  Lap 30's "the tail cell
   controls the general cell" is FALSE as stated.
4. **What the general cell actually adds** (`sum_blockCount_cellSet_le`): the length-`n` cells are
   disjoint and all inject into the tail cell under ONE shift, so their frequencies SUM to at most
   the tail's; the Gauss masses sum the same way.  The crux is exactly "the tail cell's visits are
   spread across the length-`n` words in proportion to `γ(I_w)`".
5. **The threshold is free** (`orbitCellBound_of_orbitWordBound`).  Split the cell into
   `⋃_{T ≤ a ≤ S} I_{w++[a]} ∪ cellSet w (S+1)`, kill the residual with (3) plus
   `tailFreq_le_of_levyBound` and `tendsto_freeRate`, hand the finitely many cylinders to the
   `T = 1` hypothesis.  This is the payoff of laps 38–42.
6. **`ImageTight`, not `LevyBound`.**  Only tightness is used, and it is the right notion:
   `imageTight_of_isCFNormal` proves CF-normality gives it (exact partition of the orbit clock +
   an EXACT covering `Ioo 0 1 ⊆ (⋃_{a≤n} I_{[a]}) ∪ Ioo 0 (2/n)`, rationals included, no null
   sets), whereas `LevyBound` needs uniform integrability that normality does NOT supply.
7. **A cylinder is an interval** (`sub_le_gaussMeasure_cfCylinder`, `orbitWordBound_of_orbitACBound`):
   `M − m ≤ 2 log 2 · γ(I_w)`, and irrational orbit points never hit the rational endpoints.

## ⚠️ Two obstructions, recorded so they are not re-attacked

* **`ImageTight` on the IMAGE is a genuine second obligation.**  It does not follow from the
  one-sided `OrbitWordBound` (upper bounds alone cannot stop mass escaping to `0`;
  `aᵢ = 2^{2^i}` makes every word frequency vanish).  Nor from the Diophantine route:
  `largeDigitCount_le_goodDenCountPrim` + `goodDenCountPrim_le_log` need `log qₚ(y) = O(p)`,
  i.e. a Lévy bound for the image.  And the expansions decouple: `|qₙφx − φpₙ| ≤ φ/qₙ` has
  `φpₙ ∉ ℤ`.  The clock (`Oℓ ≈ ΦPₙ`, `det Φ = φ` fixed) is the route, and it is a real build.
* **CF-normality does NOT imply a Lévy bound** (sparse enormous digits move no cell frequency
  while blowing up `∑ log aᵢ`), so any plan routing through Lévy for `x` is dead.
