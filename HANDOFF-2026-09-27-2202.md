## 🧠 Context to carry forward

The crux this lap was the **common-offset digit identity**, the last open obligation of §6 —
not the tail control (lap C) and not the encoder (earlier). The structural insight that
unlocked it: the repo already had `CastingOut.floor_lambertVal_mul_pow`, but that lemma takes
a floor and therefore *discards exactly the fractional data a digit reading needs*. The fix
was a new pre-floor split, `lambertVal_mul_pow_eq`:
`lambertVal b w * b^N = (integer head) + Σ'_j w(N+1+j)/b^(j+1)`. Everything else followed
mechanically. If a future lap needs to read digits of any Lambert-type constant, reach for
this lemma, not the floor version.

Two deliberate design choices, both cheaper than the paper's literal prescription (and both
now staged as KB insights):

- **`c := ∏ b ∈ S, b` instead of `lcm(bases)`.** The proof needs only *some* common multiple
  with `2 ≤ c`; `Finset.lcm` drags in a nonvanishing side condition (`b ∣ 0` for all `b`, so
  divisibility alone doesn't exclude `lcm = 0`) that costs more than the crude modulus saves.
  The operator instruction explicitly permitted "a proved equivalent common multiple".
- **`ε := 1 / ∏ b ∈ S, b ^ lengths b` instead of `Finset.inf'` over coordinate gaps.** One
  `Finset.single_le_prod'` gives `b^{lengths b} ≤ P`, hence `ε/2 ≤ (1/2)/b^{lengths b}` in
  every coordinate simultaneously, and the empty-set case needs no `Nonempty` threaded in.

Interior cylinders chosen as `(v/b^ℓ, (v+1/2)/b^ℓ)` — strictly inside the digit cylinder
`[v/b^ℓ, (v+1)/b^ℓ)`, leaving margin `1/2` for the carry. That is why `ε` only has to beat
`1/b^ℓ`, not something depending on the word.

No dead ends worth recording: the plan derived from the paper's §6 went through on the first
attempt, and the only friction was mathlib naming (see Gotchas).
