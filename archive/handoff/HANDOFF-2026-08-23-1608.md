## ⚠️ Gotchas
- **`mul_le_mul_right'`/`mul_le_mul_left'` do NOT resolve in this mathlib
  pin** (v4.33.1 toolchain) — use `gcongr`. ENNReal cancellation lemmas are
  `ENNReal.mul_le_mul_iff_left/right` (`Data/ENNReal/Operations.lean`).
- `ENNReal.tsum_prod` won't unify through an equiv application: first
  `tsum_congr` the body into `f c.1 c.2` form, and give `f :=` with
  **explicit binder types** or the coercions make Lean infer `ℕ × List ℕ`.
- `set x := e with h` then `rw [h]` inside a lambda-position goal fails
  (needs beta); use `simp only [h]`.
- `push_neg` is deprecated in this pin (warning; `push Not` is the new
  form) — still works.
