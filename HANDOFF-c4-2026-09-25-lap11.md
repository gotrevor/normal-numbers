uniform law (no digit multiplicities): the swap preserves the one-count of the pair `{a,a+1}`.
Steps:
1. `sum_forced`: for a forced assignment `w` of the four special bits and an output-bit function
   `h`, `∑_{d : bits match w} X^{∑_{u∈I} outbit u} = X^{e}(1+X)^{m}2^{q−4−m}` with
